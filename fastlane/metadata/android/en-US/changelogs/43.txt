Contents, in the menu, lists a PDF's own outline and a Word document's headings with the page each is on, as it does a book's chapters, and goes to the one you tap. Videos and audio in a folder you added can be deleted along with photos, and Delete for good deletes them outright instead of moving them to Android's bin. Night mode works in .doc, .odt and .rtf files. Text size now sizes text files, code, subtitles and Markdown too.
