PDF pages are sharper at the screen's width, and a PDF can be pinched smaller than the screen, into one column of small pages, with a double tap to bring them back. A photo opened from a folder you added swipes to the folder's other photos, turns with Rotate and, from Android 11, can be moved to Android's bin. A tap on a photo or a track hides the title bar, as on a video. A tap on the side of a book's page moves it a screen. A presentation full of photos needs much less memory to open.
